Use the skill in `skill\blender-hair-shapes` for this, and only that skill for the hair. Read its SKILL.md first and follow it, and use its `scripts\hair_shapes.py`. Don't use any other hair skill or method.

From scratch, create the hairstyle in the hair reference images on the provided head. Everything is in your refs folder:
- `hair\`: the hair reference images. They show the front, both 45s, both sides, the back and a braid close-up, all drawn on this same head. The braids are low pigtails gathered at the back of the head.
- `hair_reference.jpg`: the original photo they were made from.
- `Hime_Head.obj`: the head, with `Hime_Head.mtl` and its texture `T_Hime_Head_BaseColor.png`.

What matters most to the person:
- Visible stragglers and baby hair.
- Real clumping, not one smooth sheet.
- Natural braid tips.

Follow the skill's order. Plan the sections, block the style out as shapes, and check the blockout against every reference view before converting it to hair.

In your out folder, include for every reference view:
- the blockout beside the reference ("before");
- the finished hair beside the reference ("after");
- a before-and-after sheet for each view.

Also include close-ups of the hairline, a clump and a braid tip, and the .blend.
