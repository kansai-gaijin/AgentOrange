# Milestones fold away from their headers, a closed one still says how far along it is, and
# finished tasks are left out unless asked for (#116). Run milestones-again.txt after it, with
# the same data folder, to see that's all kept across a restart.
# 30 tasks under 3 milestones: Milestone 1's are all done, and every third task is done.
seed "Milestone project" 30 3 0
open "Milestone project"
click "Tasks"
# Finished, so it starts closed, saying so.
expect "Milestone 1"
expect "9 of 9 done"
expectnot "Task 1"
# Open ones leave out their finished tasks.
expect "Task 2"
expect "Task 3"
expectnot "Task 13"
shot milestones
# Closed from its header, it still says how far along it is.
click "Milestone 2"
expectnot "Task 2"
expect "0 of 9 done"
shot milestone-2-closed
# Task 10 has no milestone, and is done.
expectnot "Task 10"
click "Show finished tasks"
expect "Task 10"
expect "Hide finished"
click "Milestone 1"
expect "Task 1"
expect "Task 13"
shot finished-shown
click "Milestone 1"
expectnot "Task 1"
